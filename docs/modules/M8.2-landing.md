# M8.2: Landing page

## In one sentence
The site used to open straight on the hack story. It now opens on a normal product home page: a 3D night sky drawn from the **real Euler trace**, what Unmask does, the four real hacks checked against the public record, and a clear way into the case file. The story moved to **How it works** (`/how-it-works`).

## Why
Feedback: the website began with the problem statement and didn't look like a normal website. A judge should understand in a few seconds what Unmask is, see that it works on real data, and have a button to try it.

## How it's built
- [src/landing/Landing.tsx](../../frontend/src/landing/Landing.tsx): the page and every scroll animation. **Lenis** smooth scrolling drives **GSAP ScrollTrigger** from one clock (`useSmoothScroll` in `lib/motion.ts`, shared with the story). Scroll writes into one mutable object (`reveal`, `dive`, `step`), so scrolling never re-renders React.
- [src/landing/Sky.tsx](../../frontend/src/landing/Sky.tsx): **three.js** through React Three Fiber. One `Points` object with a custom shader (glow, per-kind brightness per step, the exchange pulse), the money paths as `LineSegments`, and background dust. The four tags are HTML positioned by projecting their wallets every frame.
- [src/landing/euler-sky.json](../../frontend/src/landing/euler-sky.json) (35 KB): a snapshot of the real Euler trace: every address, its kind, its volume and a 3D position from a `networkx` spring layout. Shipped with the site, so the hero appears instantly even while the free backend is asleep. To regenerate it, load the Euler replay, take `/api/graph` + `/api/money-now` and the transfers, and lay them out in 3D with `networkx.spring_layout(dim=3, seed=7)`.
- [src/landing/landing.css](../../frontend/src/landing/landing.css): the same Latent colours and fonts, brightened for the night sky.
- No new libraries: gsap, lenis, three and @react-three/fiber were already installed.

## Links into the case file
- `/case?hack=euler-2023` (any replay id) loads that real hack straight away.
- `/case?trace=1` opens the **Trace any address** form.

## Accessibility and phones
- With **reduce motion** on: no intro, no drift, nothing pinned; the three steps and the letter are shown one after another.
- The headline has a real `aria-label`; the decorative sky and tags are `aria-hidden`; the data-source strip has the full list as its label.
- On phones the graph sits in the space above the headline, the tags are hidden (they would cover the text), and the cards stack. No sideways scrolling at 375 px (checked).

## Verified in the browser
Desktop (1440 × 900) and phone (375 × 812): the intro, all three pinned steps, the cards, the grid, the closing section and the footer. **Trace a real hack** loaded Euler in the case file ($191.6M); **Paste an address** opened the form; the story works at `/how-it-works`. No console errors.

Not checked in a browser: the reduced-motion layout (the CSS and JS paths exist, but the test browser can't switch the setting).

## Limits
- The hero is a snapshot of one case. It doesn't change when another case is loaded in the case file.
- The numbers on the four cards are written into the page from [data/replays/README.md](../../data/replays/README.md). If a trace changes, update both.
