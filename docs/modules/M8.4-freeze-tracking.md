# M8.4: Freeze-request tracking

## In one sentence
Unmask already drafted a freeze letter for every party that can freeze the money. Now the dossier also says **how to send** each letter, lets the analyst record what happened (**sent → they replied → frozen**, or **declined**), writes every change to the audit chain, and moves confirmed money out of "can still be frozen" into a **Frozen** total.

## Why
A judge's obvious question was "you drafted the letter, then what?" No software can freeze crypto from outside:

| Where the money is | Who can freeze it |
|---|---|
| An exchange deposit | The exchange |
| USDT | Tether |
| USDC | Circle |
| ETH or DAI in a private wallet | Nobody |

What Unmask can do honestly is make the request fast to send and keep an exact record of what each party did.

## What the analyst sees

**Dossier → Freeze requests.** Each letter now has:
- **Who and how much:** "To Tether $10.5M".
- **A status:** *Draft: not sent yet*, *Sent, waiting for a reply*, *They are looking at it*, *Frozen*, or *Declined*, with the analyst's name and the time. The letter's colour follows its status: grey draft, gold waiting, green frozen, red declined.
- **"How to send this to …":** short guidance for that kind of party:
  - **Exchanges:** use the security-incident or law-enforcement channel published on the exchange's own website; include the deposit address and transaction hashes; ask them to freeze the account and keep the identity (KYC) and IP records. Exchanges act fastest on a police request.
  - **Tether and Circle:** they blacklist addresses, usually on a law-enforcement request. File a police report first, then send the letter through the contact published on their official website.
  - **Everyone:** during an active hack, crypto security incident-response groups (for example SEAL 911) can help reach exchanges and issuers quickly.

  No email addresses or phone numbers are invented. The guidance points to each company's own published channel.
- **Four buttons:** *Mark as sent*, *They replied*, *Funds frozen*, *They declined*.

**Case file → money panel.**
- "Can still be frozen" counts only money that isn't frozen yet.
- A green **"Frozen (confirmed in the dossier)"** row appears, with a striped green part in the bar.
- "Nobody can freeze" never changes because of a freeze: frozen money isn't counted as anyone else's.

Every status change is written to the **tamper-evident audit chain** as `freeze_requested`, `freeze_acknowledged`, `freeze_frozen` or `freeze_declined`, under the analyst's name. The dossier's "Record of decisions" includes them.

Freeze records belong to one case: loading another case starts with none.

## API

| Endpoint | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/freeze/{party}/{status}` | `status` is `requested`, `acknowledged`, `frozen` or `declined`; `party` is a name from the dossier ("Tether", "Circle", or an exchange like "Binance"). 400 for an unknown status, 404 if this case has no freezable money there. Uses the `X-Analyst` header for the audit record. |
| `GET /api/report` | Each freeze request now carries `usd`, `kind` (exchange or issuer), `how` (the guidance lines) and `state` (status, time, by whom, or none) |
| `GET /api/money-now` | New `frozen_usd`, plus `buckets`: still open at exchanges, still open with the issuer, frozen, and nobody. `frozen_possible_usd` now excludes frozen money. |

## Verified
- **Tests:** 199 passing. New ones cover the full sent → frozen flow (totals, buckets, audit check), a bad status, an unknown party, and a new case clearing the records.
- **Test stack, simulated attack 7:**
  - Three letters (Exch-A $9.3M, Tether $10.5M, Exch-B $12M), each "Draft: not sent yet" with its guidance.
  - Tether: *Mark as sent* → "Sent, waiting for a reply · analyst, 03:47 UTC"; *Funds frozen* → green "Frozen".
  - Money panel: "can still be frozen" went from $31.8M to $21.2M, "Freezable by the issuer" to $0, a new "Frozen $10.5M" row, and "Nobody can freeze" stayed at $10.1M.
  - The audit chain had 2 entries and checked out.

## Limits
- Sending is manual, on purpose: Unmask never contacts a company by itself, because an automatic accusation must not go out.
- The status is what the analyst records; Unmask can't see whether a company really froze the funds. (USDT and USDC blacklists are on-chain, so checking them automatically is a possible later step.)
