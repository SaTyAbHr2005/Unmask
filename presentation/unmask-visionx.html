Handling uncertainty and edge cases

Bots mimic market noise
Bot score: timing test, round amounts, Benford's law
Fake tokens, poisoning
Priced by real contract: Euler $272M → true $191.6M
Flash loans
Only wallets loaded whole count as holding money
Researchers quote the address
Public addresses don't count: 23% → baseline
One small cash-out
No prediction until ≥5% of the money shows it
API errors, rate limits
Retry + backoff; errors never cached
No public trail at all
Names nobody: real hacks show no suspect
Emails and IPs found
Masked until a reason is recorded

When Unmask is wrong

Suspected, not accused
Analyst confirms or rejects
Nothing sent without approval
Every decision in a tamper-evident audit chain
Confidence halves after a week
